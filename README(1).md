# Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022** is an interactive Excel dashboard created to analyze sales and order performance for an e-commerce store.

The dashboard provides a clear view of **sales, orders, customer demographics, order status, top-performing states, and sales channels**. It uses Excel charts, PivotTables/PivotCharts, and slicers to make the analysis interactive and easy to understand.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Compare sales and orders over time.
- Understand sales contribution by gender.
- Analyze different order statuses.
- Identify the top-performing states by sales.
- Compare orders across age groups and gender.
- Analyze the contribution of different sales channels.
- Create an interactive and user-friendly Excel dashboard.

## 📌 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Orders vs Sales
The dashboard compares the **amount of sales** with the **number of orders**, helping understand overall business performance.

### 2. Sales: Men vs Women
The gender-wise sales chart shows that:
- **Women contribute approximately 61% of sales.**
- **Men contribute approximately 39% of sales.**

### 3. Order Status
The order-status analysis shows:
- **Delivered:** 94%
- **Cancelled:** 3%
- **Returned:** 2%
- **Refunded:** 1%

This helps evaluate successful deliveries and identify the proportion of orders requiring further attention.

### 4. Sales: Top 5 States
The dashboard compares sales across the top-performing states. The displayed leading states include:
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh
- Tamil Nadu
- Telangana

**Maharashtra** has the highest sales among the displayed top five states.

### 5. Orders: Age vs Gender
Orders are analyzed across:
- Adult
- Senior
- Teenager

The chart also compares **Men vs Women** within each age group, helping understand customer demographics.

### 6. Orders: Channels
The channel-wise order chart shows the contribution of major sales channels:
- Amazon – 35%
- Myntra – 24%
- Flipkart – 21%
- Meesho – 5%
- Others – 4%
- Na – 5%
- Ajio – 6%

**Amazon** is the largest displayed order channel.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Women account for the larger share of sales, at approximately **61%**.
- The majority of orders, approximately **94%**, are delivered successfully.
- **Maharashtra** is the leading state among the top five states shown in the dashboard.
- **Amazon** contributes the largest share of orders among the listed channels.
- The age-and-gender analysis shows that the **Adult** customer group has the highest order contribution in the displayed analysis.
- The dashboard combines sales, order, demographic, geographic, and channel-level analysis in one place.

## 🎛️ Interactive Features
